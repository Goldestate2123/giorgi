Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Do I need to provide cleaning products?

Yes. The customer supplies cleaning products and equipment unless we agree otherwise in advance.

Do I pay through this website?

No. We send bank account details and payment instructions on WhatsApp after reviewing the request. Never send bank passwords or PINs.

Is my booking confirmed immediately?

No. Sending a request is not a confirmed booking. We confirm availability, scope, final price and payment terms with you.

Can I cancel or change a booking?

Contact us on WhatsApp as early as possible. The final cancellation and rescheduling policy will be provided before your booking is accepted.

Are monthly plans automatic subscriptions?

Not on this demo website. Monthly plans are requests only, and recurring payment arrangements must be agreed separately and clearly.

What areas do you cover?

Initial service area: Dublin and nearby areas, subject to availability. Enter your area or Eircode in the booking request.
